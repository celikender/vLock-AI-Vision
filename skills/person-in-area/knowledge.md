- "person" is the most reliable SAM word on these cameras; prefer it to "worker" or "operator".
- For hands near a machine, use the object "hand" and a small area around the danger point.
- 0.5-1 s confirmation avoids flicker from people walking past the edge of the area.
- Posters, screens, reflections and mannequins can look like a person. Use the
  person-confirmed skill when false alarms matter.
- SAM can miss people who are mostly hidden, in very low light, or seen from directly above.
